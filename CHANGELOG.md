## [0.1.0-preview.1] - Research Preview

### Added
- Initial public snapshot of the TheoremOS website ([theoremos.top](https://theoremos.top/)) and bilingual product documentation.
- Public archive of 26 Lean proof records (10 with official Lean-Eval pass records; 16 OpenAI Math records checked via our project's Lean verifier and problem comparators, with official OpenAI results not yet confirmed).
- Verification guides in English ([`docs/VERIFY.md`](docs/VERIFY.md)) and Chinese ([`docs/VERIFY.zh-CN.md`](docs/VERIFY.zh-CN.md)), featuring a Mathlib-free Lean starter example and reproduction steps for the Dimitrov official frozen workspace.
- Bilingual access guide ([`docs/ACCESS.md`](docs/ACCESS.md)) detailing the Cloud Workbench whitelist application and sign-in flow (BYOK with Responses API) and contact information for CLI/component research collaboration.
- Preset 4-step Lean verification walkthrough assets (`assets/proof-replay-en.gif` and `assets/proof-replay-zh.gif`) and cloud workbench screenshots.
- Public materials licensing guide ([`docs/PUBLIC-MATERIALS.md`](docs/PUBLIC-MATERIALS.md)), repository [`LICENSE`](LICENSE), and [`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff).
- GitHub Issue forms for early access requests, bug reports, feature requests, and general inquiries.
